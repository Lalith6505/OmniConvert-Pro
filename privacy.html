OFFICIAL PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Privacy Policy for OmniConvert Pro

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

OmniConvert Pro ("the App") is developed and maintained by Lalith Madanayake ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user privacy and data handling.

1. 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

OmniConvert Pro is engineered from the ground up as a completely private, offline, standalone utility. The App does not collect, store, transmit, process, or sell any personal information, device data, location data, or usage metrics.

2. Zero Network Activity & Zero Tracking

  • No Internet Permission: The App does not declare or request Android's android.permission.INTERNET. It is physically incapable of making network connections.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: There are zero advertising frameworks, analytics suites, or third-party trackers integrated into the App.
  • No Account Required: You never need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.

3. Local Device Storage (Calculation Tape)

The calculation tape history and user preferences (such as selected decimal precision) are stored solely on your local device using Android's private SQLite / Jetpack DataStore sandbox. This data never leaves your device and is wiped if you uninstall the App or clear app storage.

4. Children's Privacy

OmniConvert Pro does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. It is suitable for professionals, engineers, maritime officers, students, and general users of all ages.
